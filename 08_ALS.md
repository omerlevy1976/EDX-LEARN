# Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

## Core Findings

Lower Motor Neuron:

- Fibrillations
- Positive sharp waves
- Fasciculations

Chronic Reinnervation:

- Large MUAPs
- Long duration MUAPs

Recruitment:

- Reduced

---

## NCS

Sensory studies usually normal.

---

## Important Differential

Multifocal Motor Neuropathy

Look for:

- Conduction block
