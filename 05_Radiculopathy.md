# Radiculopathy

## Key Principle

Sensory NCS are usually normal.

Reason:

Lesion is proximal to the dorsal root ganglion.

---

## Diagnostic Criteria

Needle EMG abnormalities in:

- Two muscles
- Same root
- Different peripheral nerves

---

## Important Findings

- Fibrillations
- PSW
- Reduced recruitment

---

## Paraspinal Muscles

Very important.

Abnormal paraspinals support root disease.
