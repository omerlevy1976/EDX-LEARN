# Needle EMG

## Insertional Activity

Normal:

Short burst during needle movement.

---

## Increased Insertional Activity

Suggests membrane instability.

Seen with:

- Denervation
- Myopathy

---

## Fibrillation Potentials

Meaning:

Denervation

Seen in:

- Radiculopathy
- Polyneuropathy
- ALS

---

## Positive Sharp Waves

Almost equivalent to fibrillations.

Also indicate denervation.

---

## Fasciculations

Single motor unit discharge.

Can be normal.

Concerning when associated with:

- Fibrillations
- ALS

---

## Myotonic Discharges

Features:

- Wax and wane
- Dive bomber sound

---

## Recruitment

Reduced Recruitment

Suggests:

- Axonal loss
- Neuropathy

Early Recruitment

Suggests:

- Myopathy

---
