# Myopathy

## Typical Findings

- Small MUAPs
- Short duration
- Early recruitment

---

# Myasthenia Gravis

Mechanism:

Postsynaptic disorder.

Finding:

Decrement on repetitive stimulation.

---

# Lambert-Eaton

Mechanism:

Presynaptic disorder.

Finding:

Increment after exercise.

---

# Botulism

Presynaptic blockade of acetylcholine release.
