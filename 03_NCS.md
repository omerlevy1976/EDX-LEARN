# Nerve Conduction Studies

## SNAP

Sensory Nerve Action Potential

Evaluates sensory fibers.

---

## CMAP

Compound Muscle Action Potential

Evaluates motor fibers.

---

## H-Reflex

Equivalent of Achilles reflex.

Useful for:

- S1 Radiculopathy

---

## F-Wave

Assesses proximal nerve conduction.

Useful for:

- Polyneuropathies

Not highly sensitive for radiculopathy.

---

## A-Wave

Abnormal late response.

Usually pathologic.

---
