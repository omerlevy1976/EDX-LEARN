# Polyneuropathy

## Typical Pattern

- Distal
- Symmetric
- Sensory worse than motor

---

## Demyelinating Neuropathy

Findings:

- Slow conduction velocity
- Prolonged distal latency
- Abnormal F-wave

Examples:

- CIDP
- Guillain-Barre

---

## Axonal Neuropathy

Findings:

- Low amplitudes
- Fibrillations

Examples:

- Diabetic neuropathy
- Alcoholic neuropathy
