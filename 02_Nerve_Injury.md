# Nerve Injury Classification

## Neurapraxia

Characteristics:

- Conduction block
- Demyelination
- No axonal loss
- No fibrillation potentials

Recovery:

- Weeks to months

---

## Axonotmesis

Characteristics:

- Axonal disruption
- Wallerian degeneration
- Fibrillations present

Recovery:

- Requires regeneration

---

## Neurotmesis

Characteristics:

- Complete nerve disruption

Treatment:

- Often surgical

---
