# EDX Basics

## What is EDX?

Electrodiagnostic Medicine is an extension of the clinical history and physical examination.

Components:

- Nerve Conduction Studies (NCS)
- Electromyography (EMG)

Goals:

- Diagnosis
- Prognosis
- Treatment planning

---

## Key Rule

EDX is NOT a screening test.

It is a targeted extension of the neurological examination.
